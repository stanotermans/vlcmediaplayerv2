# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and respectful to others
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Assuming good intentions and helping newcomers

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@stanotermans](https://github.com/stanotermans)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct.

To report a problem, open an issue, or contact the maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/stanotermans) if the matter is sensitive. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and fairly.

Depending on the situation, the maintainer may respond with a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
