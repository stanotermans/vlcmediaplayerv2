Privacy policy

Applies to Vela Player for Android (Google Play) and for Windows, macOS and Linux. Last updated: 30 October 2026.

In short

Vela Player plays the videos and music on your own device. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect, sell or share personal data. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your library (the files in your media folders), playlists, bookmarks, where you left off and recently played.
  • Your settings, and thumbnails of your videos.

This is stored in the app's own storage and removed when you uninstall the app. On Android you can also clear it in Settings → Apps → Vela Player → Storage.

Access to your media

On Android the app asks for permission to read your videos and music (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO). It only reads them to show and play them; nothing is uploaded.

When the app uses the internet

  • Streams and links you open are loaded from the address you entered.
  • Subtitles from OpenSubtitles (only when you search for them): the app sends the file's name or title and a hash of the video file (a number computed from its contents, not the file itself) to OpenSubtitles. If you log in to your OpenSubtitles account, your user name and password go to OpenSubtitles only; the app keeps the login token on your device.
  • Vela Player Pro (Android) is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; the app only learns whether Pro was bought. To make sure a purchase is real, the app sends its Google Play purchase code (no name, email address or other personal details) to our purchase check at vela-verify.stanotermans.workers.dev (Cloudflare), which asks Google about that purchase and answers "bought" or "not bought". The purchase code is not stored or used for anything else. This only happens when Pro is bought or restored, and about once a week after that.
  • Update check (Windows, macOS and Linux only; on Android Google Play updates the app): the app asks GitHub for the latest version number. You can turn this off in Settings → About.

Children

Vela Player is a general media player and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes and contact
